Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ProCash Manager. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google Auth, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture to create your account.
  • Financial Data: Your income, expense details, and categories entered into the app are stored securely.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide expense tracking, data sync across devices via Firebase, and personal analytics. We never sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption protocols (SHA-256 for PINs and Firebase Security Rules for database operations) to protect your information.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via email support.
